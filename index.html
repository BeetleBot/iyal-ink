What is Fountain?

Fountain is a simple plain-text syntax that lets you write screenplays in any text editor. It formats automatically into industry-standard scripts without locking your writing inside proprietary file formats.

Learn more about the universal open format at fountain.io ↗

INT. CHARLES'S APARTMENT - DAY Charles sits at a desk, typing on his computer.
David sits behind him on a couch with his laptop. CHARLES (to David) I think I'm going to write this in Fountain. DAVID Never heard of it. CHARLES I think you're going to like it.

Which Tool Fits Your Workflow?

Both tools write standard Fountain screenplays. Choose the experience that suits your style.

Choose FountTUI if...

You write in terminal windows, want ultra-fast keyboard navigation, and love Catppuccin themes with zero visual distraction.

Choose ActOne Screenplay if...

You prefer a modern graphical desktop app with visual storyboards, color markers, ambient focus audio, and production breakdown tagging.
