RELEASE NOTES / V0.4.7

What's new

  • Fix Formatting — one command that tidies up your script: it collapses extra blank lines between dialogue, keeps exactly one blank line between elements, preserves multi-line action paragraphs, trims stray spaces after formatting symbols, and cleans up inline notes.
  • Cleanup summary — after Fix Formatting runs, a quick summary tells you exactly what was tidied: how many lines were removed, spaces collapsed, and notes cleaned.
  • Faster startup — ActOne Screenplay now opens noticeably quicker on both Windows and Linux, with less loading lag.
  • Updated welcome screen — the app now greets you with "Welcome To ActOne Screenplay!" and its About window has a fresh studio-style look with a link to iyal.ink.
  • Steadier editor view — the editor no longer jumps or scrolls when you open or close windows, so you always stay where you were writing.
  • Simplified settings — the old Low Power / Battery Saver mode has been removed, keeping your settings focused on what matters.
  • New help guide article — a dedicated Fix Formatting guide has been added to the in-app Help Guide.
