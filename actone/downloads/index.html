RELEASE NOTES / V0.4.9

What's new

  • Smarter AI Script Assistant — Muse AI can now directly read, edit, and suggest changes to your screenplay. Ask it to rewrite dialogue, adjust scenes, or update character details in your X-Ray panel, and preview all suggestions in clear visual cards before applying them.
  • Sleek New Right-Click Menus — Enjoy clean, modern context menus across the editor, tabs, and snapshots panel with faster response, full keyboard navigation, and smooth submenus.
  • Instant Scene & Character Navigation — Real-time script analysis lets you jump between scenes, locations, and character lines instantly without any lag, even in long screenplays.
  • Stutter-Free Typing & Smart Auto-Save — Write without interruption! Auto-save now detects when you are actively writing and waits until you take a natural typing pause before saving in the background.
  • Smoother Scrolling & Better Cursor Focus — Highlight text without erratic screen jumping, and enjoy an updated high-visibility cursor that makes it easy to track your line in both light and dark themes.
