01 NATIVE FOUNTAIN MANUSCRIPT ENGINE AUTO-FORMATTING · US LETTER & A4
01 · MANUSCRIPT TYPOGRAPHY

Authentic US Letter & A4 Paper Formatting

ActOne renders your screenplay in pixel-perfect Courier Prime on authentic US Letter or A4 manuscript paper with real-time Fountain syntax coloring. Write continuously without fiddling with margins.

  • Intelligent Tab Cycling: Press Tab to cycle through character names, parentheticals, and scene headers automatically.
  • Autocomplete Suggestions: Ghost-text autocompletion for recurring characters, locations, and transitions.
  • Plain Text Portability: 100% Fountain plain text format. Git-diffable, future-proof, and open forever.

Map your story from 30,000 feet

04 · DRAMATIC METRICS

Structural rhythm and pacing diagnostics

Gain deep insight into your story's dramatic pulse. X-Ray visualizes dialogue vs. action density, tracks character dialogue presence, and computes screen runtime.

  • Pacing Density Histogram: Track dialogue vs. action balance across all three acts.
