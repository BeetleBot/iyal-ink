THE DESKTOP WORKSPACE

From first line
to final export.

01

Native Fountain Screenplay Editing

  • Live Formatting & Highlighting: Built-in CodeMirror 6 engine with real-time Fountain syntax coloring and clean manuscript rendering.
  • Smart Formatting Tools: Automatic line-spacing, Tab-to-cycle Fountain prefixes (@, ., >), and auto-closing wrylies/parentheticals.
  • Character & Location Autocomplete: Real-time ghost text suggestions for character names, locations, and parentheticals.
Native Fountain Editor Screenshot PlaceholderCodeMirror 6 editor & syntax highlighting
02

Comprehensive Screenplay Workspace

  • Outline Navigator: Collapsible hierarchical tree view of sections (#), scenes, and synopses (=) with scene color dots and storyline badges.
  • Drag-and-Drop Scene Reordering: Reorder entire screenplay scenes directly in the Outline Navigator while preserving formatting.
  • Multi-Script Bundles (.actone): Store multiple screenplay drafts, notes, character data, and tasks in a unified ZIP archive.
  • Document Notepad & Text Parking: Dedicated sidebar for freeform outline notes and a temporary text clipboard to park snippets while editing.
Workspace & Outline Navigator Screenshot PlaceholderTree view, multi-script manager & parking
03

Writing Focus & Productivity Tools

  • Distraction-Free Zen Mode: One-key toggle (Ctrl+Alt+Enter) to hide all UI bars for full manuscript focus.
  • Typewriter & Focus Mode: Vertical center-scrolling cursor option with single-line focus fading out non-active text.
  • Writing Sprint Timer: Integrated countdown timer with live WPM calculation, sprint session history, and personal best leaderboards.
  • Automatic Snapshots & Rollback: Continuous version history with manual, auto-interval, and save-triggered snapshots.
Focus & Zen Mode Screenshot PlaceholderTypewriter mode & sprint timer interface
04

Production & Analysis Suite

  • X-Ray Screenplay Analyzer: Live analytical dashboard for Dialogue vs. Action ratios, day/night distributions, scene timing, and pacing charts.
  • Character Network & Co-Occurrence Graph: Interactive circular graph and matrix heatmaps for character interaction analysis.
  • Tag Manager & Breakdown Reports: Categorize elements into 15 production categories (Cast, Props, VFX, SFX, Camera, Extras) with CSV export.
  • Structure Templates: 8 pre-loaded story beat sheets (Three-Act, Save the Cat!, Hero's Journey, Dan Harmon's Circle, etc.).
X-Ray Analyzer & Production Breakdown PlaceholderNetwork co-occurrence graph & tag reports
05

AI Writing Assistant — Muse

  • Connect Your Own Model: Bring OpenAI-compatible API keys or connect to local Ollama instances for complete privacy.
  • Specialized @ Commands: Use @write-scene, @q (ask questions about your script), @lookup, and @synonyms directly inside chat.
  • Context-Aware Responses: Muse reads your screenplay context to offer feedback, generate Fountain blocks, and refine scenes.
Muse AI Assistant Panel PlaceholderInteractive chat & @write-scene preview
06

Professional Export & Customization

  • High-Quality PDF Engine: Powered by Rust (krilla + cosmic-text) with bold headings, scene numbers, watermarks, and orphan/widow protection.
  • Final Draft XML (.fdx) & Fountain Export: Full compatibility with Final Draft and Fade In with scene color preservation.
  • 17 Custom Built-in Themes: Adaptive light/dark themes (Catppuccin, Pitch e-ink, Sunset, Forest, Mint) plus custom theme builder.
