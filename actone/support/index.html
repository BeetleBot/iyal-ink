Frequently Asked Questions


What operating systems does ActOne Screenplay support?

ActOne Screenplay is engineered natively for Windows 10/11 and Linux distributions. High-performance builds are available on our downloads page.

Is my writing private and stored locally?

Yes, 100%. All your screenplays, notes, character cards, and projects are stored strictly on your local machine. ActOne Screenplay does not store your creative content on external cloud servers.

How do I export my screenplay to PDF or Fountain?

Press Ctrl+P (or select File → Export) inside ActOne Screenplay. Our built-in Rust export engine generates industry-standard PDF formatted screenplays instantly.

What is the .actone file format?

An .actone file is a standard ZIP container storing your plain-text .fountain script along with workspace metadata, character cards, and notes. You can extract it using any standard zip tool anytime to access raw Fountain files.
