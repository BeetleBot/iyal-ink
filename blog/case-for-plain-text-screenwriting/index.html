The Case for
Plain Text Screenwriting

Discover why moving away from proprietary file formats and adopting plain text can revolutionize your screenwriting workflow and future-proof your art.

For decades, the screenwriting software industry has been dominated by proprietary file formats. These closed systems often trap your creative work inside specialized applications, meaning you can only access your scripts if you continue paying for the software.

Plain text represents a fundamental departure from this model. Because plain text is universal, it can be opened by any text editor on any operating system, today, tomorrow, and fifty years from now.

Future-Proofing Your Art

When you write in plain text, you are ensuring that your work will outlive the software used to create it. You aren't beholden to the longevity of a specific company or the whims of their pricing models.

Furthermore, plain text files are incredibly lightweight. They sync instantaneously, take up negligible storage space, and can be easily managed with robust version control systems like Git. This enables collaborative workflows that were previously impossible with binary files.

"Your screenplay shouldn't require a decoder ring to read."

By embracing plain text, screenwriters can focus entirely on the words on the page, knowing their files are resilient, portable, and entirely theirs. Dedicated native tools that understand plain text screenwriting can bridge the gap between simple text editing and professional industry formatting.
