Protecting Your
Unproduced Scripts

Essential security practices for screenwriters to ensure their intellectual property remains safe from leaks and unauthorized access.

In an industry where a leaked script can derail a production or compromise a sale, securing your unproduced work is paramount. While registering your script with the WGA or copyright office is essential for legal protection, proactive digital security is your first line of defense.

The weakest link in digital security is often convenience. Automatically syncing your entire script folder to a public cloud service might save time, but it introduces massive vulnerabilities.

The Offline Sanctum

The most effective way to protect a script is to keep it offline. By utilizing software that operates entirely locally—without requiring an internet connection or a mandatory cloud login—you drastically reduce the attack surface for potential leaks.

When you do need to create backups, consider encrypted local drives or self-hosted, end-to-end encrypted cloud solutions. Never email a script to yourself as an unencrypted attachment.

"Your screenplay is your currency. Guard it with the paranoia it deserves."

By adopting a security-minded workflow and relying on tools that respect your privacy by design, you ensure that your stories remain yours until you are ready to share them with the world.
