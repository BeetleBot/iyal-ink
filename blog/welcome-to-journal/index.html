Building for Screenwriters

Whether it's terminal-based screenplay tools like FountTUI or native desktop workspaces like ActOne Screenplay, our focus is on building fast, distraction-free software that respects standard text formats like Fountain.

  • Local-first: Your screenplays and files stay safely on your local disk.
  • Fountain standard: Open plain-text screenplay markup with zero lock-in.
  • Focused workspace: Minimalist tools that step out of your way while you write.
