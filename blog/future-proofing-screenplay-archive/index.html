AUGUST 08, 2026 / ARCHIVING

Future-Proofing Your
Archive

Strategies for ensuring your life's work remains accessible and editable decades into the future.

Writers generate an enormous amount of text over a lifetime. Scripts, outlines, character bios, and scrapped scenes form a digital archive of your creative evolution. But how do you ensure that this archive remains accessible in twenty, thirty, or fifty years?

Relying on the proprietary file formats of today's dominant software companies is a gamble. Software ecosystems rise and fall. File formats are deprecated. Subscription servers are shut down.

The Plain Text Guarantee

The only true way to future-proof an archive is to rely on universal, open standards. Storing your work as plain text, specifically utilizing intuitive markup languages like Fountain, guarantees legibility in perpetuity.

Plain text requires no special decoder, no active subscription, and no proprietary operating system. It is the most resilient digital format in existence.

"Your legacy should not be tied to the lifespan of a software corporation."

By organizing your archive locally, backing it up redundantly, and committing to open-source file standards, you ensure that your stories will be readable for generations to come, completely independent of whatever technological paradigms the future holds.
